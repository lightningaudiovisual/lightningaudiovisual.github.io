General information about crewing for us

Currently our job roles are freelance ones.

You will be asked to invoice at the end of the month.

Our wages are the minimum wage for your age in the UK.

We have a system in place where you can pick your shifts so they are flexible around you.

Your shift is over when the job is over - everyone stays behind until we are done. You will be paid extra if this is the case.


Our minimum requirements are:

• You must have basic PPE. This includes steel toe cap shoes and a hard hat. These can be supplied by us upon request (email).

• You must be 'competent' to do the job. This can be proved through any relivant qualifications / certifications. This will be reviewed upon application.

• You are on time, every time.


If you have any questions or queries about working with us, please feel free to contact us.
