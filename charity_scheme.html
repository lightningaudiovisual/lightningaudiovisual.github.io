We understand that technical production should be integrated seamlessly with your ideas. This is why we provide a level of service which is accessible for questions and queries, with the technical know-how for your needs.

What is the Charity Scheme?

The charity scheme is designed to make audio-visual services more accessible to event organisers and organisations by providing affordable quotes.

This does not mean that quality is reduced as Lightning Audio Visual operates on the idea that ‘less is more’.

Instead of receiving a traditional quote, we take a commission from the event. This is usually 12% of your revenue.

We do not cover any losses that your event may generate.

Why have we set up our Charity Scheme?

We are passionate about making our audio-visual services accessible to everyone. This means that Lightning Audio Visual is dedicated to making this possible, in the most efficient way for you, our customer.

Our overheads are lower than our competitors because we have external jobs, which are our main source of income. This allows us to offer you this scheme.

Who is the Charity Scheme aimed at?

Charities

Smaller bands and acts

Local government initiatives and organised events

Small, independent businesses
